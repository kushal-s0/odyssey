Use `start.cmd`, the existing privately provisioned Supabase accounts and the latest verified worker. Open localhost:5173. Keep synthetic demo content labelled. Prepare a previously processed real example for slower PDF/audio paths; identify it as saved processing with its actual version/model/source provenance. Do not imply a queued upload already has a result.

Use [the 18-file rehearsal pack](../input/README.md) for fictional positive/negative inputs and fact keys. Select Synthetic rehearsal input and reuse its scenario family across formats. Graphs exclude these examples by default; deliberately enable labelled examples to show them. Demonstrate grouped bars, completeness-versus-rating scatter, accessible table/CSV and the single-assessment radar. Explain evidence confidence with actual review flags, not a success probability. A missing radar dimension is absent, never a zero score.

1. **0:00–0:40 — Applicant and input.** Sign in, switch dark/light mode and show text/PDF/audio input plus recording/file preview. Submit a short invented tea-business idea. Explain that the laptop handles one model stage at a time and the queue persists.
2. **0:40–1:40 — Saved evidence and assessment.** Open a completed example, compare original and English evidence, missing/ambiguous fields, six potential dimensions, separate completeness/coverage and the source-review gate. Explain that unknown is not zero and a self-reported claim is not independent validation.
3. **1:40–2:20 — Explain and calculate.** Show financial checks using supplied units/periods and request the configured Groq explanation. If quota/provider is unavailable, show its honest fallback. Scores remain server-computed. Show OSM only for a confirmed approximate area, with date/attribution and incomplete coverage.
4. **2:20–3:10 — Improve a version.** Show three localized follow-up questions, English originals, typed/audio response and saved prior versions. Use an already completed follow-up to compare facts, coverage and completeness; do not promise higher potential.
5. **3:10–4:10 — Assessor.** Use an assigned assessor account, inspect scoped queue/source evidence, record a reasoned outcome and show transcript correction history. Show authorized related ideas and explain cosine similarity is neither plagiarism probability nor success likelihood.
6. **4:10–5:00 — Compare and report.** Show the four views, real denominators, synthetic exclusions and small-group warnings. Print/export the selected historical report. Close with the actual remaining limits: handwritten failures, human language validation and unverified public deployment. Do not claim the relevance experiment was promoted.
