Tata STRIVE business idea screening

Udyam Saathi

From a business idea
to an informed first review.

A practical guide to every main feature, the underlying workflow, recorded model results and a five-minute demonstration.

The 30-second explanation

An applicant can describe a business through text, PDF or voice. Udyam Saathi organizes the original evidence, prepares English text, extracts business facts and proposes a structured review for a mentor.

What makes it useful

Source-linked facts, visible financial warnings, focused follow-up questions and one workspace for applicants and assessors.

Who decides

The human mentor. The prototype supports investigation and mentoring; business success and funding suitability are not validated predictions.

03 / How the system works

Private input → durable processing
→ evidence → human review.

1
React / Vite interface

Supabase sign-in; applicant or assessor workspace; input, progress and saved results. Only public browser configuration is exposed.

2
FastAPI + Supabase

API validates identity, cohort access and file limits. Supabase supplies Auth, PostgreSQL records/jobs and private original storage.

3
Owned-laptop worker

Claims durable work through scoped API operations. One GPU model job at a time; checkpoints allow controlled recovery.

4
Four processing stages

Extract → translate → structure → assess. The exact extraction path depends on text, digital PDF, scan or audio.

5
Saved result and human action

Server recomputes totals and arithmetic. Reviewer sees sources, risks and questions; similarity, local context and comparisons add perspective.

StageWhat happensWhat survives for review
1. ExtractRead text layer, OCR pages or transcribe audio.Original, segments and source/page/time references.
2. TranslateIdentify language; prepare English. English passes through.Original and translated evidence, uncertainty.
3. StructureQwen proposes typed business facts with quotations.Accepted facts, conflicts and missing fields.
4. AssessPropose six domain judgments; compute deterministic checks.Reasons, coverage, rubric version and review flags.

Why jobs are durable: closing the tab does not erase an accepted submission. If the worker is offline, work stays queued instead of pretending to finish. Fenced leases prevent an old execution from overwriting a newer one.

Current operation is local UI/API + Supabase + an owned laptop. A hosted frontend/API with an outbound laptop worker is a deployment target, not an already verified public service. Foundation models are third-party open weights.

04 / Applicant studio

Submit naturally.
Keep the original intact.

Guide me

A 12-question interview in three steps: idea, market, numbers. Covers offer, customers, problem, stage, location, demand, competition, difference and finances.

Preview the assembled source. Skip unknown answers rather than inventing them.

Free text / file / voice

Paste a proposal, upload a PDF, record a voice note or upload audio. Playback helps applicants check recordings before submission.

Microphone tracks stop when leaving recording; delayed permission responses are cleaned up.

InputCurrent limitUseful explanation
Text30,000 charactersFastest way to avoid document/OCR overhead. No need for polished grammar.
PDF20 pages; 25 MiBDigital text layer first. Scanned pages need OCR and may take much longer.
Audio10 minutes; 25 MiBWAV, MP3, M4A, MP4, OGG and WebM. Transcription quality varies with language and recording conditions.

Language and locality

English / Hindi interface: menus and guidance can switch without discarding the current workspace. The interface choice is separate from the submission’s content language.

Input options include English, Hindi, Marathi, Telugu, Bengali and Odia. An adapter existing does not establish accuracy. Other-language quality remains unvalidated; English/Hindi human review is available. A coarse locality is optional and must be confirmed before nearby-business lookup.

Upload recovery and honest progress

Size, media signature, decoding/page checks, owner binding and file hashes protect intake. Reusing the same accepted request avoids duplicate jobs. Transfer pause is separate from server cancellation; resume with the same input when offered. An attached original is protected from unattached-upload discard.

“2/4” means the current stage, not 50% of time. Page/batch/domain counts and elapsed time show ongoing work. Visible tabs track for up to 30 minutes; hidden tabs pause polling. Use Refresh status after returning. Accepted work remains saved.

Use “Synthetic rehearsal input” and a scenario-family identifier for demo variants so they do not inflate ordinary comparisons. Unsent in-memory drafts survive navigation, but hard-refresh persistence is not guaranteed.

05 / Understanding and evidence

Every useful answer starts
with “Where did this come from?”

The result connects business fields back to the applicant’s own material, instead of presenting a model paragraph as verified truth.

Information groupExamples of structured fields
Business and customerBusiness type, offer, target customers, problem, current operating state.
Market and operationsLocation, demand/research, raw materials, suppliers, competition and differentiation.
MoneyInvestment, own funds, requested loan, selling price, revenue, expenses and stated profit.
Evidence statePresent, unknown, ambiguous, conflicting or unreadable; source quote and source pointer.

Original and English

Keep the source-language content beside the English evidence. This lets a reviewer notice a lost negation or an incorrect money period.

Translation is an aid; it can omit meaning. Financially important statements deserve source checking.

Source navigation

PDF: page references.
Audio: chunk time ranges.
Text: character spans / quotations.

Audio ranges identify a chunk; they are not exact word alignment.

What the validation layer contributes

  • Typed schemas and quotation checks reject unsupported structured claims.
  • Explicit numerical statements can be recovered through source-span rules.
  • Competing amounts stay as conflicts; the system does not silently select a convenient value.
  • Unknown or unreadable information remains unresolved, rather than becoming a fabricated answer.
  • Embedded instructions in a proposal are treated as input content, not permission to change scoring rules.

Source verified ≠ business verified. Confirming that a transcript matches the recording does not prove the applicant has customers, a supplier or an approved loan. Self-reported demand stays self-reported.

What to showOpen one extracted money fact, follow its source reference and explain whether the value and its period are actually supported.
06 / T03 rubric explained

A proposed rubric,
with uncertainty kept separate.

T03 defines the project’s initial scoring contracts and rules. This rubric is proposed by the project; it is not an organizer-provided formula or a validated funding predictor.

DimensionWeightWhat the reviewer investigates
Clarity15%Is the offer, customer and operating model understandable?
Customer need20%What supports the claimed customer problem and demand?
Market potential20%Is there a reachable segment and a plausible channel?
Feasibility25%Do resources, supply, funding and economics fit together?
Competition10%Are alternatives known, with a credible response?
Differentiation10%Is the difference specific and relevant to customers?

Anchors: 0 = evidenced weakness; 1 = weak support; 2 = plausible but limited; 3 = well-supported description; 4 = concrete independent validation. Current model judgments cannot claim level 4.

Potential = 100 × Σ(weight × rating ÷ 4) ÷ Σ(assessed weights)

Evidence coverage

The share of rubric weight that can be assessed. Unknown, unreadable and unresolved conflicting domains are null, never automatic zeros.

Information completeness

A separate 18-field applicable checklist. It measures usable information supplied, not business merit. Trusted exclusions need a reason.

When the headline is withheld

Below 70% coverage, unresolved critical offer/customer/financial/location facts, or source-review flags can suppress the headline. The provisional arithmetic remains available for investigation. Ranking requires all six assessable domains, no critical issue and an authorized comparable population under the same rubric.

50/100 is not a 50% chance of success. A score, coverage and completeness answer different questions. Grammar, language, accent, gender, schooling and social identity are excluded from the scoring payload; measured outcome fairness is still unvalidated.

Bars/radar summarize available ratings; missing values are omitted. A deterministic total can be mathematically correct while its underlying model judgments are poor.

Show the risk before
you discuss the score.

The first-review summary prioritizes operating loss, funding gaps, incompatible figures, source review and missing facts. It reuses saved evidence and server arithmetic; opening it does not call another model.

These are fictional quick-demo input expectations, not new measured model outputs. Files: input/quick-demo/tea-positive-en.* and tea-loss-en.*. “Constructive” means informative self-report; demand and profit are not independently verified.

What the arithmetic engine actually checks

Server-side Decimal arithmetic checks compatible revenue/expense periods, stated funding versus startup need, and price × stated units × operating days when all inputs are available. It does not assume missing currency, unit counts or working days. Requested credit is not approved financing; sales are not profit.

The honest negative-case answer: in prior testing, a loss case produced the correct −₹8,000 calculation yet a provisional model score of 50. Across four weak-domain expectations, none received the expected low rating. The prominent risk summary helps reviewers notice this; it has not repaired or revalidated semantic grading.

What to say“A negative financial result must stay visible even when the model is too optimistic. The mentor follows the evidence rather than treating the score as a decision.”
08 / Business plan and scenario tools

Turn the review into
a practical planning conversation.

Business blueprint

A six-group map organizes the current business facts and gaps. A Markdown business brief carries evidence forward into a readable plan.

This summarizes submitted information. It does not certify the claims or silently fill unknown answers.

Scenario planner

Try explicit INR assumptions for unit price, variable cost, daily sales, operating days and fixed monthly expenses; startup investment is optional.

These are scratch assumptions, kept separate from the submitted facts and assessment score.

OutputMeaning and conditions
Monthly salesUnit price × daily units × operating days.
Costs and surplusVariable costs + fixed monthly costs; sales minus these costs gives the scenario’s operating surplus.
MarginSurplus relative to sales when the denominator is valid.
Break-evenUnits needed to cover fixed costs when per-unit contribution is positive.
Simple paybackStartup investment divided by positive monthly surplus; otherwise no meaningful positive payback.
Stress testRecalculate with 20% fewer sales. Pin a scenario for comparison, edit assumptions or reset.

A small explanation example — calculated, not a model benchmark

Assume price ₹20, variable cost ₹10, 30 units/day, 25 days/month and ₹5,000 fixed costs. Sales are ₹15,000, variable costs ₹7,500 and operating surplus ₹2,500. At 20% fewer units, surplus falls to ₹1,000.

Break-even = ₹5,000 ÷ (₹20 − ₹10) = 500 units/month
With ₹20,000 startup cost: simple payback = 8 months at the base assumptions.

Planning estimates, not promises. Taxes, borrowing costs, owner wages or other missing expenses may change the result. Editing a scenario never rewrites the applicant’s saved evidence or increases a rating.

What to showChange one assumption, inspect the stress result, then return to the original facts to show that the assessment has stayed separate.
09 / Explore market

Give an idea context
without inventing the market.

Similar submitted ideas

MiniLM represents eligible business text as 384-dimensional vectors. PostgreSQL/pgvector retrieves related current submissions within the viewer’s authorized scope. The interface shows advisory matches and their similarity.

What a match can help withWhat it cannot establish
Find related business types or approaches for mentor discussion.That a similar idea is a competitor, an original invention or a proven successful business.
Compare how another authorized proposal describes customers or operations.That a high cosine similarity is a high business-quality score.
Surface relevant indexed records.That an empty result means no related idea exists. Data may not be indexed or accessible.

Access filters and scenario-family exclusions avoid revealing unrelated cohorts or showing synthetic variants as independent neighbors. The tiny curated retrieval evaluation is encouraging, but real-corpus relevance remains unvalidated.

Nearby-business context

After the applicant confirms a coarse area, the app can show OpenStreetMap business-category listings around it. Context carries the source, lookup radius, date/freshness and OpenStreetMap attribution / ODbL licensing information.

Useful question

“These mapped listings suggest alternatives nearby. What do you know about their customers and your proposed difference?”

Safe interpretation

No mapped listings means incomplete observed coverage, not “no competition.” Informal and rural businesses may be absent.

Context is optional and non-blocking. A lookup failure does not erase the assessment. A coarse confirmed area is enough; precise private GPS is not required. Do not claim that listings prove demand, footfall, sales or viability.

What to say“Similarity helps us find related submissions. Local listings help us ask better questions. Neither replaces field research.”
10 / Assessor and clarification workflow

Human review is a workflow,
not a disclaimer at the end.

1
Find the right idea

Search/filter the authorized queue by program, status and other available criteria. The assessor sees only permitted records.

2
Inspect one consolidated workspace

Review original/English evidence, domain reasons, warnings, financial checks, business map, context and history.

3
Record a reasoned outcome

Mark reviewed, request clarification, send for deeper evaluation, or recommend mentoring/skilling. Keep the explanation.

4
Correct or clarify, then reassess

A source correction or linked clarification creates a versioned result; the prior original and assessment remain traceable.

Focused next steps for the applicant

The system proposes up to three prioritized questions, with the issue, reason, hint and source context. Conflicts take priority over generic missing fields. Text or audio answers link to the existing idea and can trigger reassessment.

IssueUseful clarification
Conflicting expenses“Which monthly expense amount is correct, and what does it include?”
Unfunded startup need“How will you obtain the remaining capital? Is the requested loan approved?”
Uncertain demand“Have any potential customers agreed to a trial order or payment?”

Optional English/Hindi explanation

A user-requested Groq explanation can rephrase already-saved facts and ratings if free credentials and allowance are available. It shares those facts with an external provider, has a daily cap and does not change the score. The owned-model assessment path does not require it.

A revision is not guaranteed to score higher. Better information may expose a risk. Reviewer verification checks source fidelity; it does not independently validate the applicant’s commercial claims.

11 / Four comparison views

Compare patterns
with the denominator visible.

The comparison workspace summarizes current, authorized assessments. It helps a program notice mentoring needs; it does not demonstrate which cohort will create more successful businesses.

ViewQuestion answeredHow to explain it
DomainWhich of the six dimensions looks stronger or weaker?Dimension averages on a 0–100 presentation scale, with rated counts and missing-data context.
TradeHow do records group by the project’s controlled business-trade taxonomy?Descriptive summaries under the selected scope and rubric. Trade is a classification aid, not ground truth.
Cohort / programWhat does this authorized learner group need?Cohort means trusted membership, not a folder of language samples. Use coverage/completeness alongside scores.
Across cohortsHow do cohorts compare within the same trade?Keep trade and rubric comparable; inspect composition, counts and exclusions before drawing conclusions.

Ways to inspect

Grouped bars, a scatter view and readable tables make differences visible. CSV export supports further authorized analysis.

Sample size, rated counts, rubric version and completeness/coverage explain what is actually being summarized.

Filters that matter

Use current eligible records. Apply authorization and same-rubric rules. Synthetic examples are excluded from ordinary population charts unless explicitly included.

Scenario-family handling prevents text/PDF variants from inflating the sample.

A concrete interpretation

If a cohort has low information completeness and many unassessable need ratings, the appropriate action is to collect better customer evidence. Calling it a cohort of “bad businesses” would confuse missing information with negative evidence.

Small samples are descriptive only. These charts are not causal analysis, significance tests, fairness certification or business-success prediction. Averages inherit the limitations of the model judgments underneath them.

What to showSwitch through all four views, point to sample counts and rubric version, then explain one useful mentoring action instead of announcing a winner.

Why some tasks say “blocked”

The tracker records 13/29 done. A feature can exist while its human-quality, hosted recovery or publication acceptance gate is unfinished. This count is neither product usefulness nor model accuracy.

What is ready to explain

Private multimodal intake, structured evidence, proposed ratings, financial warnings, planning, reviews, context and comparisons. T06 intake checks are complete.

What is not established

Reliable weak-business grading; genuine handwriting/regional speech quality; independent corpus/fairness labels; full public deployment and hosted recovery.

Highest-value next improvements

  1. Have an English/Hindi business mentor label positive, weak, uncertain and conflicting examples; calibrate false optimism and abstention.

  3. After explicit publication approval, rehearse free hosting and owned-worker recovery with the actual application.

Owned hardware avoids an expiring inference-provider allowance, but has uptime, GPU, thermal, electricity and network limits. Supabase/free hosting has quotas. No unlimited, fully cloud-hosted or production-ready claim is supported.

Three likely committee questions

Did you train the foundation models? No. They are third-party pretrained models. We built the screening, grounding, review and context workflow; an experimental relevance head was trained and rejected.

How accurate is it? There is no defensible single number. Cite the component denominators on page 13 and disclose 0/4 expected low negative-domain ratings.

Can it reject applicants automatically? It is intended for mentor-assisted first review. The current quality evidence does not support autonomous rejection or funding approval.
