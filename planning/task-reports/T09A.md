## Failures and repairs

Initial hash-ordered split put the only negative family in test, leaving training
one class, so fitting correctly abstained. Conservative unknown/tea merging and
an explicit class-aware group split keep that sole minority family in train;
holdouts remain one class and cannot establish irrelevant-class generalization.
This is documented split design, not test-driven threshold tuning. No split,
labels or metric is independently reviewed. Regex QA boundaries may be wrong.
